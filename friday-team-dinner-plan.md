# Friday Team Dinner Plan

This week's team dinner is hosted for **4 people**, including one vegetarian teammate.
The menu below is drawn from the [HowToCook](https://github.com/Anduin2017/HowToCook) community cookbook (程序员在家做饭指南): the recommended dishes come from its 4-person meal recommendation, and the vegetarian options are picked from its 素菜 (vegetable dish) category.

## Recommended dishes (4 people)

The following dishes came back from HowToCook's four-person meal recommendation (3 meat dishes, 1 dessert, 1 soup):

- 啤酒鸭 (Beer Duck)
- 麻辣香锅 (Mala Hot Pot / Spicy Stir-Fry Pot)
- 蚂蚁上树 (Ants Climbing a Tree — glass noodles with minced pork)
- 酸奶意式奶冻 (Yogurt Panna Cotta)
- 金针菇汤 (Enoki Mushroom Soup)

## Vegetarian options

Picked from HowToCook's 素菜 (vegetarian) category for our vegetarian teammate:

- 地三鲜 (Di San Xian — stir-fried potato, eggplant and green pepper)
- 松仁玉米 (Sweet Corn with Pine Nuts)
- 凉拌豆腐 (Cold Tossed Tofu with Scallion and Garlic)

## Notes

- Two of the recommended dishes are already meat-free (酸奶意式奶冻 dessert and 金针菇汤 soup, made without egg), so the vegetarian teammate has dessert and soup covered.
- The vegetarian options above are fully plant-based and do not use oyster sauce.
- Feel free to comment on this file with requests or substitutions before Friday — then we can build the shopping list together.
